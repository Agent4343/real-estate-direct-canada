# Crafting a Strong Trade Quote Request

Use this template to ask contractors for detailed, comparable quotes. Fill in the brackets and remove any sections that don't apply.

## Project Snapshot (one paragraph)
- **Property:** [address/neighborhood, property type] with any access constraints (gate code, parking).
- **Goal:** [one-line outcome, e.g., "replace failing main shutoff and upgrade to code"].
- **Timeline:** [target start/finish dates] and site hours [e.g., 8a–5p, weekdays].

## Scope (bullet or table)
List measurable tasks to reduce assumptions:
- **Task + quantity:** ["Replace 2 shower valves" or "Prep/paint 480 sq ft ceiling"].
- **Materials/specs:** [brands, finish level, code requirements]; note acceptable alternates.
- **Exclusions:** [what you will handle—e.g., finish paint, drywall patching, permits].

## Site Conditions & Access
- **Status:** [occupied/vacant] and any constraints (pets, HOA rules, noise limits).
- **Utilities/staging:** [power/water availability, elevator use, dumpster location].
- **Photos/visit:** Link photos/video and offer a walkthrough to avoid change orders.

## Pricing Format Requested
Ask for clear, comparable pricing:
- Line items for **labor**, **materials**, **permits**, **disposal/cleanup**, and **taxes**.
- Identify **allowances**, **contingencies**, and any **assumptions**.
- State **quote validity period** and preferred payment schedule/milestones.

## Quality, Compliance, and Communication
- Provide **warranty terms** and proof of **insurance/licensing**.
- Share 1–2 **recent comparable projects** (photos/refs) if available.
- Confirm **point of contact** and best channel (phone/email) for questions.

## Ready-to-Send Message
> I need a licensed **[trade]** for **[specific work]** at **[address/neighborhood, property type]**. The job includes **[task list with quantities/measurements]** using **[materials/brands/specs]**; exclusions: **[list]**. Please provide a **line-item quote** (labor, materials, permits, disposal, taxes), noting **allowances**, **contingencies**, and any **assumptions**. Include **warranty terms**, **insurance/licensing**, and links to any **similar projects**. Site access is **[dates/times]**; **[walkthrough/photos]** are available. Target completion is **[deadline]**. Quotes valid for **[X] days** are appreciated—contact me at **[phone/email]** with questions.

### Filled Example (Plumbing)
> I need a licensed **plumber** to **replace the failing main shutoff and add a pressure-reducing valve** at a **single-family home in Eastwood (123 Maple Ct.)**. The job includes **(1) replacing the 1" main shutoff with a brass quarter-turn ball valve, (2) installing a PRV and setting pressure to 60 psi, (3) replacing 2 hose bibs, and (4) insulating 12 ft of exposed copper** using **Apollo/Watts or equivalent, full-port valves, type L copper, and foam insulation**; exclusions: **drywall repair/paint, landscaping, and permit fees (owner to pull)**. Please provide a **line-item quote** (labor, materials, permits, disposal, taxes), noting **allowances**, **contingencies**, and any **assumptions**. Include **warranty terms**, **insurance/licensing**, and links to any **similar projects**. Site access is **weekdays 8a–5p; walkthrough/photos** are available. Target completion is **by May 30**. Quotes valid for **10 days** are appreciated—contact me at **555-123-7890 or owner@email.com** with questions.

## Quick Checklist Before Sending
- [ ] Measurements/quantities are clear and accurate.
- [ ] Exclusions and responsibilities (permits, patch/paint, disposal) are listed.
- [ ] Requested brands/finishes are specified or acceptable alternates are listed.
- [ ] Timeline, access windows, and site constraints are stated.
- [ ] Pricing request covers taxes, disposal/cleanup, allowances, and contingencies.
- [ ] Warranty, insurance/licensing, and documentation requests are explicit.
- [ ] Photos/video links are attached and a walkthrough time is offered.

## Why This Works
- Reduces assumptions that lead to change orders.
- Standardizes quotes for easier comparison across contractors.
- Surfaces insurance/licensing early to avoid compliance issues.
- Encourages clear timelines, documentation, and site-readiness expectations.

## What to Do Next (step-by-step)
1. **Draft once, reuse often:** Save a completed version of this template for your property so you can quickly reuse it with multiple contractors.
2. **Attach proof:** Collect photo/video links, floor plans, and any spec sheets or permit notes; include them in every outreach to avoid follow-up delays.
3. **Send in batches:** Share the request with at least 3 contractors on the same day so quotes arrive within the same validity window for easy comparison.
4. **Create a comparison grid:** Log each quote’s totals, assumptions, allowances, and exclusions in a simple table (e.g., spreadsheet) to spot gaps or hidden costs.
5. **Schedule short calls:** Offer a 10–15 minute walkthrough call for top candidates to confirm scope, surface risks, and tighten timelines before committing.
6. **Lock timelines:** Ask shortlisted contractors to restate start/finish dates and validity period in writing; file these alongside their insurance/license proofs.
7. **Agree on change-order rules:** Confirm how changes will be priced (rate, approval process, documentation) before work starts to prevent surprises.

### Attachment Pack (send with every request)
- **Photos/video:** Clear shots of every area plus any prior repairs to avoid scope gaps.
- **Plans/markups:** Floor plan, simple sketch, or markup showing locations and measurements.
- **Specs:** Preferred brands/finishes, code references, or product links you will supply.
- **Constraints:** HOA rules, quiet hours, staging limits, or delivery instructions.
- **Contact sheet:** Primary/backup contacts with phone/email for access questions.

### Optional: Walkthrough Agenda (15–20 minutes)
- **Safety & logistics (2 min):** Parking, entry, staging, restroom, and power/water availability.
- **Scope review (8–10 min):** Walk each task, call out measurements, finishes, and hidden risks you suspect (e.g., prior repairs, access limits).
- **Constraints (3–4 min):** HOA/permit needs, noise windows, lead times on materials you expect.
- **Pricing & process (3–4 min):** Reiterate line-item request, expected validity window, and change-order approval steps.
- **Next steps (1–2 min):** Photos, plans, or references you will send and the target date for their detailed quote.

|---|---|---|

### Bid-Leveling Follow-Up (when quotes are close)
Send this when two or more quotes are similar but you need clarity before deciding.

> Thanks for your quote dated **[MM/DD]**. Before we decide, please clarify:
> 1) Are **taxes, permits, and disposal** included in your total? If not, please itemize.
> 2) Confirm **start/finish dates** and any lead-time risks on materials.
> 3) List **allowances, contingencies, and exclusions** you assumed.
> 4) Share **proof of insurance/licensing** and one **similar project** from the last 12 months.
> 5) Confirm you will follow the attached **change-order approval** process.

### Kickoff Day 1 Checklist
- Walk the **accepted scope** with the contractor and confirm measurements match the quote.
- Reconfirm **site rules**: access hours, parking, staging, restroom, dust/noise controls.
- Post the **change-order steps** on-site (who approves, how pricing is sent, response times).
- Verify **permits/inspections** status and who schedules inspections.
- Align on **daily/weekly updates** (channel, time of day, who attends).

## If Quotes Are Vague or Off-Target
- **Re-state the scope:** Quote back their summary of the work and compare it to your task list; highlight any missing quantities or specs.
- **Ask for clarifications:** Request line items for allowances, exclusions, and contingencies; insist on unit rates if totals feel inflated.
- **Share examples:** Provide photos or links that show the finish level or install method you expect to reduce interpretation.
- **Set a response deadline:** Give a specific date/time for a revised quote so conversations don’t stall.

## Red Flags to Watch For
- No proof of **insurance/licensing** or reluctance to share recent comparable work.
- Quotes that lump everything into one line item without assumptions, allowances, or exclusions.
- Start dates that move repeatedly before you sign, or payment terms demanding large upfront sums without milestones.
- Pushback on documenting change-order rules or warranty terms.

## When You Pick a Contractor
- **Confirm compliance:** Re-verify license/insurance and keep copies with the accepted quote and comparison grid.
- **Written acceptance:** Send a short acceptance note referencing the dated quote, scope, and agreed change-order process.
- **Payment structure:** Document deposit, milestones, retainage (if any), and what unlocks each payment.
- **Kickoff checklist:** Share site contacts, access details, staging areas, and communication cadence (e.g., daily text, weekly recap).

### Acceptance Note Template
> Thanks for your quote dated **[MM/DD]** for **[project]**. I accept the scope as written and the **[payment schedule/milestones]** with **[deposit %]**. Change orders require **written approval** with pricing and schedule impact. Please provide updated **license/insurance** copies before start. **Site access**: [days/times]. **Kickoff contact**: [name/phone/email].

### Change-Order Request Template
> Subject: Change Order Request – **[project name]**
>
> Hi **[contractor]**, please price the following change for **[project/address]**:
> - **Requested change:** [describe work and why it’s needed]
> - **Location/area:** [where on the property]
> - **Measurements/quantities:** [sq ft, linear ft, counts]
> - **Materials/specs:** [brands/finishes or "match existing"]
> - **Timing impact:** [state if critical path or deadline-sensitive]
> - **Photos/refs:** [links]
>
> Please reply with **line-item pricing** (labor, materials, permits, disposal, taxes), any **assumptions/allowances**, and the **schedule impact** (added days or date shift). No work should proceed until this change order is **approved in writing**.

### Close-Out Checklist
- Collect **as-built photos**, serial numbers, and any **warranty/maintenance notes**.
- Confirm **permit sign-offs** or final inspections are complete (attach proof).
- Ensure **punch list** items are documented, dated, and signed once resolved.
- Verify **invoicing matches milestones** and retainage (if any) before final payment.
- Store **licenses/insurance, quote, change orders, and acceptance note** together for future reference.
